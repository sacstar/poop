How to use this page

  1. Add the Attendance Tracker

    Add the Attendance Tracker Excel file as it is. The page reads each person's type (Warehouse, Surge, LSI…), crew (Days, Nights or Weekends) and daily codes. From those it knows who was scheduled each day and catches tardies (T), early outs (LE, ED) and absences (UA, UCA, NCNS). Keep the tracker up to date before running the report.

  2. Add the punches from the timeclock (recommended)

    Punches let the page check exact clock-in and clock-out times, down to the minute. In the timeclock website, run the Punch Report for the whole pay week, Sunday through Saturday. Save it as CSV or Excel. The Timecard Report for each employee also works. The hours summary (one line of totals per person) does not work, because it has no clock-in times.

  3. Only for unusual weeks: a schedule

    Normally the tracker is enough. Each person is scheduled on their crew's days (Days and Nights Mon–Thu, Weekends Fri–Sun) plus any OT day in the tracker. Their hours come from the hours column next to Shift (like "6am-4:30pm"), or the crew's standard hours if that's blank. If someone worked different hours, click Get schedule template, fill it in and add it. A schedule overrides the tracker for that person.

  4. Drop the files in

    Drag the files onto Add files, or click Choose files. You can add several at once. Each file gets a green ✓ with how many employees and which days it covers. A red box means the file couldn't be used, and it tells you why.

  5. Pick the pay week

    Choose the week next to Pay week. Weeks still in progress are marked "(in progress)". Don't pay out on those.

  6. Clear the "Needs review" list

    Click the yellow Needs review box to show only those people. Click a name to see their day-by-day punches and what needs checking. Then set Decision this week to Qualifies or Does not qualify and type the reason in the note box.

  7. Mark anyone sent home

    The timeclock can't tell if someone was sent home for performance or conduct. For anyone who was, open their name, set Does not qualify and note why, for example "Sent home for conduct 10/2".

  8. Send the results to payroll

    When nobody is left in Needs review, click Download CSV or Copy for Excel and paste it into a spreadsheet. The file lists every employee, their status, bonus amount and the reasons.

What the statuses mean

Qualifies
Worked every scheduled shift in full. Gets the flyer amount for their role and shift.
Does not qualify
Clocked in late, clocked out early, or missed a scheduled shift. The reason shows with the exact times.
Needs review
The page can't decide on its own. You make the call (see below).
Not eligible
Their department isn't covered by the flyer, such as staffing agency workers.

Why someone needs review

  • A code like EOA, LA, EA, ADA or V: approved early out, approved late, excused absence, doctor appointment or vacation. The flyer says "no exceptions", so decide each one. To treat a code the same way every time, change it in Settings › Attendance tracker codes.
  • No attendance code: the tracker is blank on a day the person's crew works. Fill in the tracker, then add it again.
  • Tracker says P but no punches: the tracker and timeclock disagree. Check which one is right.
  • No attendance tracker or schedule: the person isn't in the tracker. Check the name matches, or decide yourself.
  • Clock-in or clock-out entered by hand: a manager typed the time in. Make sure it's the real time before approving.
  • No clock-out: the person forgot to punch out, or is still at work.
  • Punch data ends before the week is over: the export doesn't cover the whole week. Export again after Saturday.
  • Worked both day and night shifts: pick the right bonus rate under Bonus rate.

Common questions

A file shows a red box. What do I do?

Read the message in the box. It says what's wrong. Most often the file is the hours summary instead of the Punch Report, or it covers the wrong dates. Export the Punch Report again and add that. Click Remove to take out the bad file.

Someone is missing from the list.

They have no punches and no scheduled shifts in the week you picked. Check that the export includes everyone (all departments) and the full week.

Someone shows the wrong role or bonus rate.

Click their name and change Role (Driver, Warehouse or Not eligible) or Bonus rate (Weekdays, Weekends or Nights). The page remembers this for future weeks. To change a whole department at once, open Settings at the bottom and use Departments.

Someone was a minute late. Is that really a "no"?

The flyer says no lates and no exceptions, so the page counts even one minute. If the company decides to allow a few minutes, change Grace minutes in Settings. Otherwise, use the Decision box for a one-off exception and write down who approved it.

What are the meal-break notes?

They point out lunches shorter than 30 minutes, or lunch starting more than 8 hours into a shift. Nevada law requires a 30-minute meal break. These notes don't affect the bonus, but a manager should follow up.

Is the pay information safe? Where do my decisions go?

The files are read only in your browser and are never uploaded. When you close the page, they're gone, so add them again next time. Your decisions, notes and settings are saved in this browser on this computer. Use the same computer and browser to pick up where you left off. Always download or copy the final report for payroll.

Can I try it without real data?

Yes. Click Load example data to see made-up employees covering every situation. Add your real files when you're ready, and the example data is replaced.
